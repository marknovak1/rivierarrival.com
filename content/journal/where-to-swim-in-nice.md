The Promenade is beautiful and the beach is pebbles. That is not a complaint so much as a planning note. Some days you want water without a strategy for your ankles.

Here is how I rank four swims within a bus ride, by how far you carry your towel:

1. **Coco Beach** — closest if you are already in the port area. Still stones, but a smaller theatre of them.
2. **Villefranche** — a proper bay, easier on the feet once you commit to the walk down, and the bus ride is half the pleasure.
3. **Cap d'Ail Mala** — further, worth it when you have the morning. Bring water. The path is the point.
4. **Passable** — when you want to feel that you left Nice without leaving the coast.

Take the bus with a ticket that actually works (I have written about that separately). Swim early in summer. And if the pebbles do defeat you, that is allowed. There is always a café at the top of the steps.
