      The rows are a long-term home, not a holiday let. Rent means an unfurnished lease, the rent itself, with building charges kept separate until I know they are included. A one-bedroom and a two-bedroom are not the same search in Nice as they are in Menton, so each town has its own lines.

      If you are deciding whether to move at all, start with [Moving to the French Riviera from the US and Canada](guide-moving-to-the-french-riviera.html). The towns, in words rather than prices, are on [Neighborhoods](neighborhoods.html).
