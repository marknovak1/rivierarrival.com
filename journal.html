The knife sharpener who comes to Libération market on Fridays

He sets up between the fishmonger and the olive stand at seven, works until the crowd thins, and charges four euros a blade. I brought him a knife I'd been ruining bread with since April. He looked at it the way a doctor looks at a patient who waited too long.
